# 🎵 Music Directory

Place your audio files here for the birthday website.

## Required Audio Files

| Filename | Used For | Recommended |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| `background.mp3` | Soft background music that plays throughout | Calm, happy instrumental |
| `celebration.mp3` | Plays when birthday candles are blown out | Upbeat, celebratory song |

## Tips
- **MP3 format** is recommended for best browser compatibility
- Keep file sizes reasonable (under 10MB each) for fast loading
- Background music should be **loopable** and not too loud
- Celebration music should be **energetic and short** (30-60 seconds ideal)
